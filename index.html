About me

I am currently a PostDoc at the Faculty of Mathematics at University of Vienna, working with Prof. Emmanuel Schertzer. I am a member of the Biomathematics Group, and a member of the research network Discrete random structures: enumeration and scaling limits.

Previously I was a PostDoc at the Institute of Discrete Mathematics and Geometry at TU Wien with Prof. Benedikt Stufler, working on limits of random networks.

I completed my PhD under the supervision of Cecilia Holmgren and Svante Janson at Uppsala University, my doctoral thesis is titled Decorating trees grown in urns.

My masters studies were completed at the University of Manitoba under the supervision of David S. Gunderson, my masters thesis is titled On partition regular systems.

My research interests lie primarily in probability, with a particular focus on random trees and random tree-like networks. I also work in biomathematics with a focus on population genetics, and in combinatorics with a focus on graph theory.
